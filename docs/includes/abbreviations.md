*[IMU]: Inertial measurement unit — the accelerometer and gyroscope (and on newer cameras a magnetometer) inside the camera
*[FOV]: Field of view
*[GS]: Global shutter
*[UVC]: USB Video Class — the camera appears as a standard USB webcam
*[NCM]: USB network mode used with iPhone
*[SDK]: Software development kit
*[UTC]: Coordinated Universal Time
*[MCAP]: An open container format for robotics data, used by Foxglove and ROS 2
*[OTG]: USB On-The-Go — lets a phone act as the USB host for the camera
*[BLE]: Bluetooth Low Energy
*[VIO]: Visual-inertial odometry
*[SLAM]: Simultaneous localization and mapping
*[AAR]: Android library archive
*[GOP]: Group of pictures — the keyframe interval of the video
