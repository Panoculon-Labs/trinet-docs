# Tested power banks

Because Trinet cameras draw very little current, some power banks mistake them for a fully charged
phone and switch off. These are banks we and our users have tried.

## Tips

- A three-camera kit draws more current than one camera, so a bank that switches off with a single
  camera may work fine with a kit.
- If your phone needs a USB-A to USB-C adapter for the bank's USB-A port, any OTG adapter works.
- Test a new bank with a full-length session before a real data collection.
