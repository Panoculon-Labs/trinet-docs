# Care and handling

## Operating conditions

- Trinet cameras are designed primarily for **indoor use in well-ventilated environments**.
- Avoid **direct sunlight** and hot environments; the camera protects itself from overheating (see
  [Heat](thermal.md)), but heat shortens recording sessions.
- Unless your model is explicitly specified otherwise, the camera is **not water- or
  dust-resistant**. Keep it away from rain, spills and dust.

## Handling

- **Lens:** keep the protective cap on when not recording. Remove it by pulling straight off. Never
  twist or screw the lens — the focus is set at the factory.
- **Cleaning:** wipe the lens with a clean microfibre cloth; don't use solvents.
- **Drops:** avoid drops onto hard surfaces.
- **Memory card:** don't insert or remove the card while recording — stop first and wait for green.

## Cables

A worn or low-grade USB-C cable is the most common cause of unexpected disconnects and restarts.

- Use the cable supplied with the camera or a good-quality USB-C cable.
- Route the cable so it isn't bent sharply or tugged by head and arm movement (see
  [Mounting](../get-started/mounting.md)).
- Replace cables that are frayed or loose in the socket.

## Storage and transport

Store the camera with the lens cap on, in a dry place out of direct sun.
