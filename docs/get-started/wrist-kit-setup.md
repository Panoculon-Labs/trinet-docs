## Out of the box

Wrist Kits are **paired at the factory**. Each camera needs a small removable radio-beacon adapter on
the back (beacons are sold separately) — keep it attached; it's what keeps the cameras in sync.

<div class="photo-row"><figure><img class="product-shot" src="../../assets/images/product/wrist-kit-beacon-attached.webp" alt="Radio-beacon adapter attached to the back of the camera" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Adapter attached — ready for kit use.</figcaption></figure><figure><img class="product-shot" src="../../assets/images/product/wrist-kit-beacon-removed.webp" alt="Radio-beacon adapter removed from the camera" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Adapter removed.</figcaption></figure></div>

<ol class="steps" markdown>
<li markdown>Insert a memory card in every camera and connect power. Each light turns
<span class="led led-green"></span> **green**.</li>
<li markdown>**Tap the button on any camera.** Its light turns <span class="led led-blue"></span> blue,
and about a second later every camera in the kit turns blue — all are recording.</li>
<li markdown>**Tap any camera again to stop.** Every camera saves its take (white) and returns to green.</li>
</ol>

If only one camera starts, check that every camera has its radio-beacon adapter attached, then
pair the cameras as below.

## Pair cameras into a kit

Use this to re-pair a kit, replace a camera or build a kit from separate cameras. All cameras must
be ready (green) with the adapter attached.

<ol class="steps" markdown>
<li markdown>**Choose the leader.** On the camera you want as the kit leader, **press and hold** the
button until its light **blinks blue** (about 3 seconds). Pairing is now open for 30 seconds.</li>
<li markdown>**Join the others.** On each other camera, **double-tap** the button. Its light blinks
green while it searches, then gives a short <span class="led led-green"></span> green flash when it
has joined — and the leader flashes green too.</li>
<li markdown>**Wait for the leader.** The leader keeps blinking blue until the 30-second window ends
(hold its button again to close it early), then returns to green.</li>
<li markdown>**Test.** Tap any camera — all of them should start together.</li>
</ol>

Pairing is saved on each camera and survives power cycles.

## Remove a camera from a kit

**Triple-tap** the button on that camera while it is ready (green). The light blinks red five
times — the camera now records on its own.

## During a kit recording

- **Any camera controls the kit.** Start or stop from whichever camera is easiest to reach.
- **If the radio link drops,** each camera keeps recording on its own and re-aligns automatically
  when the link returns. A camera powered on mid-take joins the take in progress.
- **If a camera gets too warm,** the whole kit pauses and resumes together once all cameras are
  cool. Stereo cameras show a red blink during the pause; tap to cancel it.
- **If one camera's card fills up or is removed,** the whole kit stops, so takes stay matched.

## Watch the kit from your phone

With firmware 0.5.9 or newer, the Trinet app's [Wireless status](../app/wireless-status.md) screen
shows every camera in the kit — recording, card present, take number — and can alert you if one
stops unexpectedly.
