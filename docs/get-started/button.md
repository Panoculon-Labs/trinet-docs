# Button

Trinet cameras have one button. What it does depends on the gesture and on what the camera is
doing.

| Gesture | When | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Tap** | Ready (green) | Start recording. In a kit, every camera in the kit starts together. |
| **Tap** | Recording (blue) | Stop recording. In a kit, every camera stops together. |
| **Press and hold** until the light blinks blue (about 3 seconds) | Ready (green) | Make this camera the **kit leader** and open pairing for 30 seconds. Hold again to close pairing early. |
| **Double-tap** | Ready (green) | **Join** a kit leader that is open for pairing (searches for 30 seconds). |
| **Triple-tap** | Ready (green) | **Leave** the kit (unpair). The light blinks red five times. |
| **Tap** | Kit cooling pause (Stereo, red blink) | Cancel the pause and return the kit to ready. |

Notes:

- While the light is **white** (saving or repairing), the button is ignored — wait for green.
- After a **card full** stop on Trinet Mono, the button does nothing until you replace the card and
  reconnect power.
- In **USB webcam** and **iPhone** modes, the button does nothing; recording is controlled by the
  app.
- The button **never changes the camera's mode** (since firmware 0.5.4). Modes are set by the
  memory card and the app — see [Recording modes](modes.md).
