## Contact us

**Email:** [innovate@panoculonlabs.com](mailto:innovate@panoculonlabs.com?subject=Trinet%20support)

To help us help you quickly, include:

- the camera model (Mono, Stereo, Stereo GS, Wrist Kit) and, if you know it, the
  [firmware version](../firmware/index.md#check-your-firmware-version);
- what you did, what you expected and what happened — including the
  [status light](../get-started/led-indications.md) colour and pattern;
- for recording problems, the affected files (`.mp4`, `.imu`, `.vts`) if you can share them.

**Developers:** report SDK and toolkit issues on GitHub —
[Trinet-SDK](https://github.com/Panoculon-Labs/Trinet-SDK/issues) ·
[Trinet-tools](https://github.com/Panoculon-Labs/Trinet-tools/issues).
