# Warranty

!!! info "Official terms"
    This page summarises the Trinet Limited Warranty. The binding terms are at
    **[panoculonlabs.com/trinet-warranty](https://www.panoculonlabs.com/trinet-warranty)**; the version
    in effect on your invoice date applies.

## Coverage

| | Period |
|---|---|
| Trinet camera — camera module, image sensor and lens, IMU, main board, enclosure, button, status light; for Wrist Kits, the radio sync beacon | **12 months** from the purchase invoice |
| Supplied USB cable and mounts | 90 days from the invoice |

Covered: defects in materials and workmanship under normal use, following these instructions.

## Not covered (summary)

- **Physical damage** — drops, impacts, cracked enclosures, damage to the USB port.
- **Environmental damage** — liquid or dust (unless your model is specified as resistant), corrosion,
  or use or storage outside the specified temperature ranges.
- **Lens or calibration tampering** — rotating or adjusting the factory-set lens.
- **Opening or modifying** the camera, or removing the serial number.
- **Third-party power and accessories** — damage from non-certified or fast-charging/MagSafe power
  banks, low-grade cables or incompatible cards.
- **Consumables and wear** — memory cards, cosmetic wear, straps and caps.
- **Software and data** — the SDK, toolkit and other software, and loss of recordings.
- **Thermal shutdowns** — automatic temperature protection is a safety feature, not a defect.

Calibration is covered only as described in the official terms.

## Dead on arrival

Inspect your cameras on delivery. A camera reported non-functional in writing **within 3 days** of
delivery is replaced free of charge, including shipping both ways.

## Making a claim

<ol class="steps" markdown>
<li markdown>**Do the basic checks** — a known-good cable and power source, the memory card correctly
seated, a power-cycle. [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) helps.</li>
<li markdown>**Submit a claim** using the form linked from the
[official warranty terms](https://www.panoculonlabs.com/trinet-warranty), or
[contact us](index.md).</li>
<li markdown>**Wait for your RMA number.** Don't ship a camera without one.</li>
<li markdown>**Ship it** securely packed, with the RMA number marked and **your memory card removed**.</li>
</ol>

We repair the camera or replace it with a new or factory-refurbished unit. Typical turnaround is
10–15 business days from receipt. Repaired or replacement units are covered for the rest of the
original period or 90 days, whichever is longer.
